User Manual

1. Your 4-Digit Code

After successfully adding a book to sell or request, you will receive a 4-digit code. Keep this code safe! It acts as your password to manage your listings.

2. Managing Your Books

You are currently in the Seller Management section. Here you can see the books you are selling and the ones you requested (Looking For). You can delete items that have been sold or fulfilled.

3. Approval Process

All submitted listings initially go to a Pending state. An admin will review and approve your listing, usually in less than a few minutes. You can track this status on this page.
