User Manual

1. Your 4-Digit Code

After successfully adding a book to sell or request, you will receive a 4-digit code. Keep this code safe! It acts as your password to manage your listings.

2. Managing Your Books

To view, delete, or add more books, navigate to the Home Page and click the small blue profile icon in the top right corner. This will take you to the Seller Management page. Enter your 4-digit code there.

3. Approval Process

All submitted listings initially go to a Pending state. An admin will review and approve your listing, usually in less than a few minutes.
